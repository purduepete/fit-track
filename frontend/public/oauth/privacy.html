Privacy policy

This policy covers Jim Brown's personal Fit Track application and its Google Drive importer. Contact: jim.brown1@gmail.com.

Data and purpose

The Drive connection is intended to list and download health-export files from the owner's configured folder. File names, identifiers and export contents are used to find files and import health metrics and workouts into the owner's private tracker. Depending on the export, these files can contain sensitive health and workout information.

Google authorization credentials allow the importer to access Drive on the owner's behalf. The connection is being configured; this page does not indicate that unattended Drive imports are already active.

Storage, access and sharing

Imported health records are stored in the owner's private Fit Track database. Downloaded files and processing records may also be retained on the owner's host, and source exports remain in Google Drive unless the owner removes them. Authorization credentials must be kept in protected host storage and are never published on these pages.

Google user data is used only for this personal tracking workflow. It is not sold, used for advertising, shared with advertisers, or used to train general-purpose AI models. The owner operates the application and controls access to its data.

These public pages use Google Fonts, which causes the browser to contact Google to retrieve font files. The theme preference is saved in browser local storage. These pages do not load health records or analytics trackers.

Retention and removal

Health records and exports are retained until the owner deletes them. Backups may retain earlier copies until those backups are removed or expire. Revoking Google access stops future authorized access; it does not delete files already downloaded or records already imported.

The owner can revoke the connection in Google Account connections. Contact the address above to request removal of imported records, retained files or authorization credentials.

Google API data

Fit Track's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
