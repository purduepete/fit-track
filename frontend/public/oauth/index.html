Fit Track is Jim Brown's personal application for viewing Apple Health metrics and workouts.

Why Google Drive access?

The dedicated importer is intended to retrieve health-export files from Jim's Google Drive and send them to his private Fit Track installation. This connection supports unattended imports without requiring the Google Drive desktop application to be signed in.

Google authorization is for the owner's account. This application is for personal use and does not accept public registrations.
